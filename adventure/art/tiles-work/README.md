# Terrain and prop drafts

These assets are provisional. They have not been approved as final art and the two ground tiles need more work before a production tileset can be assembled. They may be used as temporary greybox material; preserve their status and do not claim that tile transitions or a complete art family exist.

The approved style reference is `art/concepts/direction-a-selected.png`. Three original image-generation outputs are recorded in `manifest.json`; LibreSprite 1.3 cropped and reduced them to native-size drafts, and the shared LibreSprite script `scripts/clean-sprite-alpha.js` removed semi-transparent haze from the prop exports. Editable `.aseprite` files are single-layer imported masters, not fully layered illustrations. Paths and SHA-256 hashes of originals and outputs are in the manifest.

- `previews/grass-repeat.png` and `previews/paving-repeat.png` show 3×3 repeats, enlarged without smoothing. Both terrain outputs are opaque 32×32 PNGs. Grass repetition and edge cuts remain visible. Paving's strong tile boundaries produce intentional lines, though their weight and uniformity need work.
- New larger draft ground materials `outputs/grass-v3.png` and `outputs/paving-v2.png` are 128×128 with corresponding editable single-layer `.aseprite` sources. Their 3×3 previews are `previews/grass-v3-raw-repeat.png` and `previews/paving-v2-raw-repeat.png`. Grass v3 has no conspicuous repeating flowers, though clumps still repeat. Paving wrap edges align with internal grout lines; an edge must be evaluated against matching internal grout, not against the whole image's average contrast. Both have been visually reviewed only as 3×3 samples, not in the game.
- For a 32px Tiled grid, place each 128px image as an ordered 4×4 group of 32px cells and repeat the group; arbitrary cell shuffling has not been checked for seam consistency. Terrain transitions, alternate stones, broader art-direction acceptance, and mobile/in-game QA remain outstanding.
- `previews/props-contact.png` presents the four 96×128 transparent prop canvases, enlarged on a cream background. `previews/tree-preview.png` and `previews/workshop-preview.png` show two larger world objects. The associated files in `outputs/` are separate PNGs with binary alpha; the figures fit within their canvases. Their visible bases are above the canvas bottom, so position using the per-prop `suggestedBaseAnchor` from the manifest. The tree is a single sprite; an in-game layering test is needed to decide whether its canopy should be split for occlusion.
- Save → reopen → PNG export was checked for all eight `.aseprite` draft files, with identical RGBA pixels after round-trip. This does not verify layered authoring, animation, an integrated scene, or visual approval.

Run `python3 art/tiles-work/verify.py` to check source/output fingerprints, dimensions, alpha modes, crop bounds and per-tile boundary-vs-interior edge contrast. The seam contrast is diagnostic, not a pass/fail test: a grout line can legitimately have strong contrast. Always inspect repeated previews at native scale and in the actual scene.

Before production: create compatible ground transitions and edge/corner pieces, reduce repeated grass motifs, add stone variants, inspect crop edges and sprite pivots in an actual game scene, and refine or redraw as required. None of the props has yet been checked in a playable environment. Do not deploy the generated full-resolution references.
