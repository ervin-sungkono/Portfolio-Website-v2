# Town and experience review

Six GPT-6 Luna subagents reviewed the art direction, map composition, UI, mini-games, loading, and runtime performance at maximum effort. This review uses code, source art, exported files, and offline composition proofs. The final map composition was compared with the approved reference; browser/device UI, movement, FPS, and memory were not measured.

## Approved environment pack; integrated

The site owner explicitly approved the environment art pack for integration on 2026-11-12, and its terrain and grouped scenes are now integrated into gameplay. `public/art-review/manifest.json` records the approval, retains the reviewed map hash as historical provenance, and records the final integrated map hash. The gallery at `/art-review/index.html` remains available as the approved pack record. It includes four repeated terrain swatches, three construction/depth pieces, three botanical variations, three grouped place studies, four animation loops with frame stepping and native-size zoom, and an illustrative layout proposal. The final offline overview was reviewed against the approved reference: the desktop crop shows all three photo previews, and the narrow phone crop shows the fountain edge, the clear passage and the garden steps. These are static art proofs, not browser or device QA. Sources, exports, and the repeatable pipeline are documented in `art/town-redesign/README.md`.

The key visual change is composition: connected garden paths, rounded retaining walls, visible terrace faces and stairs, coordinated furniture, and clear entrances. The workshop and café sit north, the fountain plaza is central, the photo garden is immediately east, the arcade is southwest, and the river and bridge are south. The garden moved 192 pixels east to leave a 96-pixel passage beyond the complete fountain medallion, including the garden landing. All three photo previews fit the initial desktop view. Narrow phones show the garden entrance; visitors walk east to bring its paintings into view. Main streets retain 96 world pixels of clearance; the garden and café stair approaches narrow to 66 and 78 pixels to match the drawn stone openings. The northeast garden arch is 69.75 pixels wide. Browser/device movement remains unverified.

## Changes applied immediately

- `createGame.ts` removes invisible collision Zones from the display list while preserving the static physics group. This removes those entries from camera culling and depth sorting. A test uses a real Phaser display list/body/group and proves the detached Zone still blocks movement. The number of zones follows the current map, and this is not a measured FPS increase.
- `preloadAdventure.ts` releases completed/failed prewarm Image references and handlers and removes unfinished handlers on unmount. It preserves entry-art priority, prewarming order, secondary decoding, and cancellation. The focused test covers completion, errors, decoding, and disposal.
- The approved town terrain, fountain/river/tree cels, café patio, arcade nook, and photo garden are integrated. The groups use separate base art, foreground cuts, collision rectangles, and photo windows. The offline composition proof showed aligned frames, visible floor through the arch, and connected bridge stairs; it does not validate browser rendering or movement.
- The two walkers use the approved sprites and short, collision-checked circuits with landmark pauses. The artist circles between a painting and the west bench; the traveler browses both cabinets within the arcade's clear central floor. Both routes avoid the raised planters. Shallow diagonal legs suit their side-facing art; they stop their walk animation while looking around. There is no runtime pathfinding loop.
- Owner screenshots exposed gaps left by thin collision strips around raised beds. Filled wall/bed volumes now block entry from courtyard floors; redundant strips that extended into transparent stair padding were removed. Forecourts connect the streets to the actual stair centers, and the fountain medallion renders below the garden landing. New real-Phaser movement regressions use points traced from the source pictures, rather than only checking that exported rectangles match metadata. The visible-bed check fails on the previously published map and passes on the corrected map; positive movement checks keep the steps and arch open.
- `measure-release.mjs` reports initial HTML and directly referenced CSS/JS separately from the complete export and optional Phaser chunk. The README now agrees with the simplified Garden Glide controls: tap/click or Space.

## Remaining opportunities and limits

1. **Welcome-image size:** the lossless hero is about 2.45 MiB. The latest initial HTML plus directly referenced CSS/JS is 687 KiB raw / 206 KiB gzip; the full 17.38 MiB export contains 209 files, and the optional Phaser chunk is 1.34 MiB raw / 357 KiB gzip. These are file-size measurements, not network or runtime measurements. A responsive or lossy version could reduce the largest visible download but changes pixel fidelity; review an alternate before replacing the source.
2. **Fast re-entry versus graphics memory:** the paused Phaser instance is deliberately retained after Exit to avoid loading the town again. Destroying it would reduce retained graphics resources but reintroduce the loading behavior the owner asked to remove. Keep the current behavior until device measurements justify a change.
3. **Content and new modes:** the owner supplies personal facts/photos. Keep new mini-games, achievements, avatar names, and optional direction buttons outside the current map integration.

The current preloader deliberately warms town assets after the hero and later prepares dialogue/arcade assets. Deferring the arcade bundle would save requests for visitors who never play, at the cost of a colder first launch. The owner's stated preference favors prewarming, so no change was made. Four-frame cel sprites replace the previous tree tweens. Hero image size and retained Phaser graphics remain measurement candidates, not proven bottlenecks; FPS and memory still need browser/device measurement.

The approved review gallery adds reference assets to the complete static export. They are not imported or preloaded by the game and do not add to its initial asset requests. Gameplay assets live under `/game/town-v2/`; runtime URLs must not point into `/art-review/`.
